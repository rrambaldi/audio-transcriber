# A few large subjects and only what matters, with who does what and the
# questions left open repeated at the foot. Lines starting with # never
# reach the model: what it has to know, such as leaving demos and screens
# out, is in the explanations below.
Requirements: what the system has to do, and why (a legal, technical or process constraint) when that was said
Decisions: a choice actually taken, even a provisional one ("for now")
Proposals: an idea put forward and not yet decided, with how it was received if that was said
Actions: who undertook to do what, and by when if that was said
Facts: only those needed to understand a requirement or a decision, never the steps of a demo or the clicking through screens
Open questions: a question left unanswered, or a problem still to be solved
